# About

I have been working in regional climate modelling for more than 25 years, with a particular interest in a **regional Earth system approach**, coupling the different components of the regional climate system. My main geographical areas of interest are the **Euro-Mediterranean climate and the Mediterranean Sea**.

My research aims to improve our understanding of regional atmospheric and oceanic processes and how they evolve in a changing climate. My scientific interests include **thermohaline circulation and deep-water formation, Mediterranean heat and water budgets, the role of aerosols in the regional climate system, and extreme precipitation**.

An important part of my work is also devoted to **training, teaching and sharing scientific knowledge**. I have contributed to the training of many early-career researchers who now work in academic research or R&D. I teach regional climate modelling and make my courses, publications, research data, videos and interviews openly available whenever possible.

I strongly value **collaborative research**. I have participated in numerous national and international research projects and have been an active member of the **CORDEX community since its launch meeting in Toulouse in 2009**.

I am also committed to **public science that contributes to society**, particularly by supporting local adaptation to climate change. For more than ten years, I have contributed scientific expertise to national climate services in France. I also consider communication with non-specialist audiences an important part of my role as a climate scientist, helping to explain climate change and its implications for society.

More broadly, I see climate research as contributing simultaneously to **scientific knowledge, education and action**.
